Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Tabata Timer & Tracker ("the app") is operated by Sossa-DOK. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it is handled.

Data we collect

The app does not collect any personal data. No account is required to use the app.

The only data stored is your workout history (exercise names, session dates, and rep counts per round). This data is stored exclusively in your device's local storage and never leaves your device.

Data sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties. No analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools are used.

Internet access

The app requests internet access solely to load the web application on first use and to cache it for offline use. No data is sent to any server during this process.

Data deletion

You can delete all stored workout data at any time by clearing your browser or app storage in your device settings.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.

Contact
